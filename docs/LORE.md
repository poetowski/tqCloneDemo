# UNTHREADED: lore and writing

The world and its voice. Mechanics are in [GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md).

## The premise

Three sisters keep the thread of every life. **Klotho** spins it, **Lachesis**
measures it and **Atropos** cuts it with her golden shears. When the shears close, a
mortal dies and the soul goes down to Hades.

The shears have been **stolen**. Since then no thread can be cut, so nothing can truly
die. Soldiers slain in old wars claw back out of their graves as the **Unthreaded**,
hollow shades bound to the earth. Wounded beasts mend overnight. Hades starves for
souls, and the black water of the **Styx** seeps up through cracks in the world. It glows
teal where it touches the living land.

The shears did not vanish whole. They were broken into **three blades** and hidden
where the thief's allies could guard them. The first blade is in Elaion.

## The hero: the Threadless

At the very moment the shears were taken, the hero's thread was being cut. Half the cut
went through: the hero died on the beach at Elaion and then woke up. No thread binds them
now, so no fate can steer them. Klotho says only one who holds no thread can hold a
*severed* one. In the hero's hand any blade cuts the threads of the Unthreaded, and what
it cuts stays dead.

Visual signature: a **golden thread** wound around the right forearm, glowing. It whips
out in Fate's Lash and binds shades in Bound Shades. The hero is unnamed; people call them
"Threadless" and Klotho calls them "child".

## Elaion

A fishing and olive-growing village above the wine-dark sea, below a hill crowned by an
old **Sanctuary of the Moirai** that the villagers stopped climbing to. Above the
olive terraces lies the **necropolis**, where the war dead of a forgotten siege were
buried. Under the sanctuary, Hephaestus once kept a small forge, a gift to the
Fates. His bronze bull still guards it.

## Characters

| Who | What | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Klotho** (as "old Klothia") | The Spinner, disguised as an old weaver in Elaion. Quest giver. | Warm, dry, amused by mortals, speaks in thread and weaving images. Never says outright that she is a goddess. |
| **Dion the fisherman** | Villager by the well. Lost his brother twice: once to the sea, once to the sea again. | Plain, frightened, a little funny. |
| **Captain Lykos** | Champion of the Unthreaded in the necropolis, a hoplite captain from the forgotten siege. Carries the Seal of the Sanctuary. | One line: "*The line... holds.*" |
| **Khalkotauros** | Hephaestus' bronze bull, a furnace on four legs, guarding the first blade. | No words: groans of metal, a roaring forge. |
| **The thief** | Unknown in the slice. A black thread found in the bull's furnace hints at him. | (Setup for later acts.) |

## Tone

- Mythic, but grounded in sun, salt, olives and bronze. Short sentences. No modern idioms.
- Greek words where they add flavour and stay readable: *aspis*, *xiphos*, *kopis*,
  *dory*, *pithos*, *drachma*, *agora*, *stele*, *tholos*. Never a glossary dump.
- Gods are referred to the way villagers would: with caution and a bit of grievance.
- Item and skill text: one line, concrete, a little wry. *"Still warm from the forge
  that birthed the bull."*

## Key text (used in game)

- Title card: **UNTHREADED**. Under it: *A myth of the severed thread.*
- Opening line (before control is given): *"The shears closed halfway. You died on the
  sand at Elaion, and then you got up."*
- Area banners: *Elaion* · *The Olive Terraces* · *The Cypress Road* · *Necropolis of
  the Forgotten Siege* · *Sanctuary of the Moirai*.
- Death: *Your thread will not break. Hestia's hearth calls you back.*
- End card: *One blade of three. The thread runs on.* Then *Thank you for playing the
  UNTHREADED vertical slice.*
